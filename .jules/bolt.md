## 2025-02-27 - [Pandas DataFrame Filtering Optimization]
**Learning:** In pandas, switching from sequential DataFrame filtering to a simple cumulative boolean mask for outlier removal changes the underlying mathematical behavior. To optimize performance (avoiding O(N) DataFrame memory allocations) while preserving progressive filtering behavior, evaluate boolean masks cumulatively and compute quantiles on the progressively masked data.
**Action:** When filtering DataFrames in loops, avoid repeated copies and instead build a boolean mask. Apply the mask for calculations to preserve behavior, and then apply it to the DataFrame once at the end.
